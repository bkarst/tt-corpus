# tt-corpus — working notes for Claude

This repo holds Trivia Tango's documentation and marketing copy (App Store listing, GDD, GTM, branding). The implementation lives in `../tt_flutter` (Flutter client) and `../sage-next` (Next.js backend); those are canonical for features.

## Copy & writing rules

- **Never use an em dash (—) in copy.** Use periods, commas, colons, or rewrite the sentence. Applies to all App Store copy (description, subtitle, promotional text) and any user-facing marketing text. Hyphens in compound words ("turn-based", "hands-free", "world-class") are fine.
- **Don't advertise the download or call it "free."** Every App Store game already shows as a free (or priced) download, so "Download free", "Download now", etc. are redundant filler. End copy on the brand line instead.
- Brand voice is direct, confident, a little cocky ("Outsmart. Collect. Dominate."). Prefer short sentences and hard verbs; go easy on exclamation points.
